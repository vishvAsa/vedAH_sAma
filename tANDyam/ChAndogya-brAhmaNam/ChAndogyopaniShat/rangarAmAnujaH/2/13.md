## मिथुने वामदेव्योपासनम्

****


<details open><summary>विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः</summary>

उपमन्त्रयते स हिङ्कारः, ज्ञपयते स प्रस्तावः, स्त्रिया सह शेते सउद्गीथः, प्रतिर्स्त्री सह शेते स प्रतिहारः, कालं गच्छति तन्निधनम् , पारं गच्छतितन्निधनम् । एतद्वामदेव्यं मिथुने प्रोतम् ॥ १ ॥
</details>

**प्र. –उपमन्त्रयते स हिङ्कारः** । **उपमन्त्रणं** – सङ्केतकरणम् । **ज्ञपयते स**प्रस्तावः । **ज्ञपनं** – तोषणम् । मरणतोषणानिशामनेषु मित्वात् ज्ञा धातोर्हस्वः । **स्त्रिया सह शेते स उद्गीथः** । एकपर्यङ्कोपवेशनं सहशयनम् । **प्रतिस्त्री सह शेते सप्रतिहारः** । स्त्रिया आभिमुख्येन शयनम् इत्यर्थः । **कालं……निधनम्** । मैथुननिवृत्तिःइत्यर्थः ॥

By "न काञ्चन परिहरेत्, तद् व्रतम्", otherwise prohibited sex allowed in vAmadevya practice as per shankara and rangarAmAnuja. 

Job of a commentator is indeed to explain what the text says, not to twist it to suit vulgar tastes. veda is not for people who can't deal with what it says honestly, and feel the compulsion to bowdlerize and twist it. Just like shambuka-vadha in rAmAyaNa. In both cases, people uncomfortable with what a text says cope by making various excuses. Is this clear?

madhva restricts "कामपि" to one's wives.  
This is contrary to the shruti and smRti-s, which in gosava rite enjoins even incest in no uncertain terms (presumably restricting the adhikAra for the rite to those for whom incest would be impossible due to their distance being enforced by others).  
Here too, if it be presumed that the "vow itself acts as a broadcasted invitation for adultery", that "it'll tear the fabric of society" or if muslim women wanting to make little muslims line up, the prospective vratin lives in a society where he can't believe that the said vrata yields the said fruit. So he has no adhikAra for the vrata itself. So, there is no scope for twisting the rules of the vrata based on one's biases.  


This does not equate to madhva's interpretation - the key to adhikAra is the prospective's belief in the power of the vidhi to yield the fruit. Once he believes, desires, has all other the qualifications and becomes an adhikArI, he must not refuse anyone, even a mlechCha strI or a courtesan or even a "parastrI".   

In any case, "न काञ्चन परिहरेत्, तद् व्रतम्" is not intrinsically opposed to the phala - "महान् प्रजया पशुभिर् भवति, महान् कीर्त्या". A person born from a socially deprecated union can still bring glory to his faultless father, to the extant such a person can.  
For example, vyAsa produced the great shUdra vidura from AmbikA's dAsI. The yajamAna can similarly produce excellent offspring from whoever. The phala-shruti satisfied. The bad karma (deception or approaching for scorned union) is someone else's in either case; vyAsa and the yajamAna remain faultless, operating under visheSha-dharmas.

If mAdhva logic is sound, why do vyAsatIrtha's tarkatANDava etc. justify killing animals in yAgas, "बहु-स्मृति-विरोध" quoted by vedesha-bhixu being equal in both cases?

इति व्याख्यानं परस्त्री-राग-पार-वश्येन कृतम्
